2019 documentary short

Peaks and Valleys

The Search for Ryan Shtuka

In February 2018, 20-year-old Ryan Shtuka disappeared from Sun Peaks, British Columbia. This 20-minute film follows the search, the people who kept looking, and a community that would not let him be forgotten.

About the film

A search documentary

Made with TELUS Storyhive support by Allan McVicar, Jared Featherstone, and Russell Walton — three Kamloops filmmakers working as Past 11 Productions — Peaks and Valleys gathers interviews with family, friends, searchers, and others close to the investigation, plus archival moments from Ryan’s life, into a short public account of what happened after he went missing.
